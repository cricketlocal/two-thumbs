How to Play

👆

Top thumb — Wizard

Flick-swipe to cast — no aim line, so the defender can’t read your path. Faster flick = more power. Spells rebound off the rails like air hockey.

🛡️

Bottom thumb — Defender

Drag your paddle-shield. Block and it grows +10%. Miss and it shrinks −10% while the castle wall takes damage.

🔄

Role swap

After each round, attackers become defenders. Best of 3 — or Endless until the wall falls.

✨

Power-ups

Grab glowing orbs mid-field for multi-shot, freeze, repair, giant shield, and more.
